## Putin Signs Law Prohibiting the Expulsion of Foreigners Involved in Special Military Operations
On March 23rd, 2023, President Vladimir Putin signed a law that prohibits the expulsion of foreigners and stateless persons who serve in the Russian military and have participated in combat operations from Russia for administrative violations. Instead of expulsion, penalties will include fines or mandatory work from 100 to 200 hours. [7]

**COMMENT:** This law may strengthen recruitment efforts for foreign nationals into the Russian military by reducing the risks associated with administrative offenses.
