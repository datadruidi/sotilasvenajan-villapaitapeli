## Bailiffs Seize 32 Billion Rubles from SGC

On March 16th, 2026, bailiffs in Moscow announced the seizure of 32.1 billion rubles from the gold mining company SGC as part of a court order issued by the Moscow City Court. This order stems from a ruling mandating SGC to transfer all undistributed profits from 2025 and previous periods to a court deposit account. Following the announcement, SGC's stock saw a significant decline of nearly 19%, correcting to around 11% by the close of trading. [1]

## Progress MS-31 Cargo Ship Flooded in Pacific Ocean After Deorbit

On March 16th, 2026, the Progress MS-31 cargo ship was reported flooded in the South Pacific Ocean after being undocked from the International Space Station (ISS). Roscosmos confirmed that the ship, operational since July 2025, was deorbited and collapsed upon re-entering the atmosphere. Unburnt debris from the ship fell into the non-navigable ocean.
