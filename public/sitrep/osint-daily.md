## Agricultural Land Transfer for Tourism Simplified by State Duma Amendments

On March 17th, 2026, the State Duma of Russia passed amendments in the first reading aimed at simplifying the transfer of agricultural land to facilitate rural tourism development. The proposed bill, introduced in September 2025 by a group of deputies, allows agricultural land not currently in use to be reclassified as recreational land for tourism projects based on territorial planning documents and requires prior coordination with the Ministry of Agriculture.
