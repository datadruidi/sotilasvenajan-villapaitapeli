## FAS Warns Apple of Potential 4 Billion Ruble Fine

Failure to comply may result in a fine of up to 4 billion rubles due to perceived discrimination against Russian search engines. [1]

**COMMENT:** This regulatory action reflects ongoing tensions between Russian authorities and foreign tech companies over compliance with local laws. ## Russia's FAS Issues Warning to Apple Over Discriminatory Practices
On July 1st, 2026, the Federal Antimonopoly Service (FAS) of Russia issued a warning to Apple requiring the company to remove discriminatory practices against domestic search engines and to comply with regulations for pre-installing Russian software on iOS devices by July 15th. The warning was prompted by concerns that a foreign search engine was set as the default on these devices, limiting user options. [7]

**COMMENT:** This warning underscores the Russian government's ongoing efforts to enforce digital sovereignty and control over technology firms operating in the country. If Apple fails to comply, it could face significant penalties, which may influence its operational strategies in Russia.
