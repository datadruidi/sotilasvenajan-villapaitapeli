## 6. Sijamuotopeli / Case Practice

### Taivutusaineisto / Inflection data

[OpenCorpora](https://opencorpora.org/), OpenCorpora contributors. Dictionary revision 417150, corpus revision 4580142. Processed using [pymorphy3](https://github.com/no-plagiarism/pymorphy3) 2.0.6 and pymorphy3-dicts-ru 2.4.417150.4580142.

OpenCorpora-derived inflection data is available under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). [OpenCorpora licensing information](https://opencorpora.org/wiki/FAQ). The inflection selection, filtering and connection to the game's vocabulary were made for this module. Existing vocabulary translations retain their existing source attribution.

Taivutusaineisto perustuu OpenCorpora-sanastoon. Pelissä käytetään vain sanakirjasta löytyviä substantiiveja, joille on tarkistettu kuusi sijamuotoa kummassakin luvussa. Epävarmat merkitykset, taipumattomat sanat ja lukurajoitteiset sanat on rajattu pois.

Inflections come from the OpenCorpora dictionary. Only dictionary-backed nouns with verified six-case paradigms in both numbers are included. Ambiguous senses, indeclinables and number-restricted nouns are excluded.
